Our Story

Two nurses, one quiet promise: care first.

REN Rejuvenation Bar opened in February 2022, founded by two registered nurses who wanted to build something on their own terms rather than join a chain. From the first appointment, the goal has stayed the same. Listen closely, treat conservatively, and help every patient feel more like themselves.

Our care is overseen by Dr. David Christianson, MD, and every treatment plan starts with an honest conversation about what you want and what will actually serve you. No pressure, no upselling, just clinical judgment and a steady hand.
